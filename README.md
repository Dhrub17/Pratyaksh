Tips for good demo footage
- Mount the phone or camera high in a corner, the way CCTV is installed (2.5–3 m, looking down ~30°).
- Record 3–5 minutes per clip at 720p. Have people sit, type, walk in late, leave early.
- Count the real number of people yourself; that is your ground truth for the accuracy report.
- Record one clip with the equipment in view and one where you move an item out of frame.
- Get everyone's consent before recording, and don't upload raw clips anywhere public.
